Privacy Policy

Last updated November 10, 2026

Ummah shares updates, rooms and events for our MSA. You don't need an account to use it, and we collect as little as we can. This page explains exactly what that is.

The short version

  • No accounts for readers. We never ask for your name, email, phone number or school ID.
  • Notifications are optional. They are only offered in the installed app, and only turn on if you say yes.
  • No ads, no analytics, no selling data.

What we don't collect

  • Your name, email address or phone number.
  • Your location. The campus map is a static picture and doesn't use your location.
  • Advertising or analytics data. We don't use trackers or ad networks.

Feedback

Ummah no longer collects feedback. Feedback sent through earlier versions was anonymous and was never shown publicly. Only executive board members can read it, and they can delete it on request.

Notifications

Notifications use a service called OneSignal. If you choose to enable them, OneSignal receives an anonymous identifier for your device and browser so it can deliver alerts. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device or browser settings, and we'll stop sending them to that device.

Services we rely on

Ummah is built with a few outside services. They may receive technical data such as your IP address when your device connects to them.

  • Supabase stores the updates, any earlier feedback and executive accounts, and serves the data to the app.
  • OneSignal delivers push notifications to people who opt in. Its script also loads when the site opens.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typeface used on this site.

We don't sell your data or share it with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

Storage on your device

The app saves its own files on your device so it loads faster and works with a weak connection. It also remembers small choices, such as dismissing a banner. Executive board members stay signed in using a login token saved on their device. You can clear all of this from your browser settings at any time.

How long we keep things

Posts and any earlier feedback are kept for as long as they are useful to the MSA, and the executive board can delete them at any time. Notification subscriptions are kept until you turn notifications off or remove the app.

Your choices

  • Use the site without giving us any personal information at all.
  • Turn notifications off in your device or browser settings.
  • Clear the app's stored data in your browser settings.
  • Ask an executive board member to remove any earlier feedback that contains personal details. Because feedback was anonymous, we can't search for it by person, so tell us roughly what it said and which post it was on.

Students and families

This app is meant for students, families and the school community. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone through the reader pages. If you believe personal information was submitted by mistake, contact an executive board member and we'll remove it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Ummah handles data, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions or removal requests? Talk to any member of the MSA executive board.
